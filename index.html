Research Interests

My research focuses on AI Safety and Security, with a long-term vision of securing AI systems and using AI to secure the systems around them. As AI evolves from standalone models into autonomous agents capable of reasoning, planning, and acting in real-world environments, I aim to develop methods that make these systems both secure and security-capable. My research spans two complementary directions:

  • Security of AI: Understanding, evaluating, and mitigating safety and security risks in AI models and agents, including backdoors, adversarial manipulation, hidden policies, sandbagging, reward hacking, scheming, and other forms of misalignment. I am particularly interested in frontier AI preparedness, studying how dangerous capabilities and behavioral tendencies emerge, remain hidden, or manifest across different conditions, and how they can be reliably identified, controlled, and mitigated.
  • AI for Security: Developing autonomous, reliable, and verifiable AI agents that can actively identify, investigate, and mitigate safety and security risks in complex environments. My interests include cybersecurity, threat detection, risk monitoring, vulnerability analysis, and automated remediation, with a particular emphasis on long-horizon reasoning, planning, tool use, executable feedback, and verification.

Across these directions, post-training and control serve as a common technical foundation. My broader goal is to advance AI systems that are not only robust against adversarial threats and unintended behaviors but also capable of actively improving the security of the environments in which they operate.
